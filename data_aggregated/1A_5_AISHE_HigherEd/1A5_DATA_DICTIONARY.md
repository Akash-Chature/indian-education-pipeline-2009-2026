# Stage 1A.5 Data Dictionary — AISHE Higher Education Audit (2011–12 to 2023–24)

**Release Gate:** **CLEARED FOR PUBLIC GITHUB RELEASE** (`11` analytical CSVs + `1` QA log; `0` IEEE-754 float noise, `0` duplicates, `0` series-table double-counting).

### Global Methodological & Provenance Conventions
- **Anti-Double-Counting Architecture:** Every metric draws from exactly ONE authoritative `(source_table, response_type)` per academic year (`T-1/T-4/T-9` Universe for institution counts; `T-6` Actual+Estimated for headline enrolment; `T-6a/b/c/d` for Regular/Distance/Private/Online modes; `T-14` Estimated for SC/ST/OBC; `T-26/27/28/29/30` matched University-Dept scope for 12-Tier University Types & INI PTR). Multi-year series tables (`T-40..T-52`) are strictly excluded.
- **Population Projection Break (`2021–22`) & Tamil Nadu Normalization:** AISHE switched `18–23` projected population denominators from Census 2011 projections (`142.33M` in `2019–20`) to revised 2020 projections (`152.44M` in `2021–22`, `149.88M` in `2023–24`). Raw `'Tamilnadu'` spellings in `dim_population` (`2019–24`) were normalized to `'Tamil Nadu'` so South Zone and Typology rollups have `0` missing populations.
- **Addendum & Scope Disclosures:** (1) `foreign_mode_disagg_available = 0` — AISHE 2011–24 (verified in raw `2021–22` & `2023–24` Excel sheets `16FS–18FS`) does not split foreign students by Regular vs. Distance/Online or University Type; (2) `outturn_mode_disagg_available = 0` — `fact_outturn` reports `mode_of_study = 'Total'` only; (3) AISHE omits tuition fees, so the 4-Channel Structural Shift links to Stage `1A.4` (NSSO 71st) in Stage `1B`; (4) District-level AISHE data (`T-40`, `640` districts) exists strictly for `2011–12`.
