# Stage 1A.1 Data Dictionary — Macro-Financial Lakehouse (Union Budget & RBI State Finances, FY 2009–10 to 2025–26)

### Global Methodological & Governance Notes
- **Release Gate:** **CLEARED FOR PUBLIC GITHUB RELEASE** (`10` hardened analytical CSVs; `0` float-noise or duplicate failures).
- **Net Consolidation Rule (Zero Double-Counting):** `consolidated_net_nominal_cr = (union_net_total_cr − union_grants_3601_3602_cr) + rbi_31state_ed_total_cr`. Union outlays are strictly Net of `PSK`/`MUSK` reserve-fund recoveries.
- **Year Column Convention:** `academic_year` stores Indian Financial Years (April–March, `2009-10` to `2025-26`) for join compatibility with UDISE+ and AISHE.
- **Structural Breaks:** Pre-`2014-15` SSA/MDM society transfers bypassed State Treasuries (`2202` → `3601` break in `2014-15`); pre-`2017-18` Plan/Non-Plan capital columns cannot be directly compared to post-`2017-18` Revenue/Capital columns; ratios such as `utilization_pct_actual_vs_be` (`<=201.3%`), `loans_6202_to_cap_4202_pct` (`<=302.5%`), and `reco_ratio_2202_over_4202` validly exceed `100%`.
