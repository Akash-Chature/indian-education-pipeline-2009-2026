# Stage 1A.1 Data Dictionary — Macro-Financial Lakehouse (Union Budget & RBI State Finances, FY 2009–10 to 2025–26)

### Global Methodological & Governance Notes
- **Net Consolidation Rule (Zero Double-Counting):** `consolidated_net_nominal_cr = (union_net_total_cr − union_grants_3601_3602_cr) + rbi_31state_ed_total_cr`. Union outlays are strictly Net of `PSK`/`MUSK` reserve-fund recoveries.
- **Year Column Convention:** `academic_year` stores Indian Financial Years (April–March, `2009-10` to `2025-26`) for join compatibility with UDISE+ and AISHE.
- **Structural Breaks:** Pre-`2014-15` SSA/MDM society transfers bypassed State Treasuries (`2202` → `3601` break in `2014-15`); pre-`2017-18` Plan/Non-Plan capital columns cannot be directly compared to post-`2017-18` Revenue/Capital columns; ratios such as `utilization_pct_actual_vs_be` (`<=201.3%`), `loans_6202_to_cap_4202_pct` (`<=302.5%`), and `reco_ratio_2202_over_4202` validly exceed `100%`.

### Acronyms & Key Concepts Quick Reference
| Term / Abbreviation | Plain-English Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Actual / RE / BE** | Union/State budget stages: **Actual** = final audited spending; **RE** = mid-year Revised Estimate; **BE** = initial Budget Estimate announcement. |
| **R&D (`rd`)** | Research & Development outlays (tracked across Ministry of Education Digital/NEP/R&D, DST, and NITI Aayog AIM). |
| **MoE / MSDE / DST / NITI AIM** | Ministry of Education; Ministry of Skill Development & Entrepreneurship; Department of Science & Technology; NITI Aayog Atal Innovation Mission. |
| **Major Heads (`2202`, `2203`, `3601`, `3602`, `4202`, `6202`)** | Indian government accounting heads: `2202` General Education Revenue, `2203` Technical Education Revenue, `3601`/`3602` Central Grants to States/UTs, `4202` Education Capital Outlay, `6202` Education Loans. |
| **PSK / MUSK** | Prarambhik Shiksha Kosh & Madhyamik/Uchchatar Shiksha Kosh — dedicated education cess reserve funds whose recoveries are netted out to prevent double-counting. |
| **Nominal vs. Real (`2011–12` / `2022–23` Base)** | **Nominal** = face-value Rupees of that year; **Real** = inflation-adjusted constant Rupees using the GDP or CPI deflator. |
| **GDP / NSDP / CAGR** | Gross Domestic Product; Net State Domestic Product; Compound Annual Growth Rate (annualized % growth). |
| **Blank (`NULL`)** | Missing or not-applicable cell (never equal to zero `0`). Exact blank counts and reasons are documented in Column 8. |
