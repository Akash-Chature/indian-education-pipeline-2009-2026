# Stage 1A.4 Data Dictionary — NSSO 71st Round Education Demand Audit (Schedule 25.2, Jan–Jun 2014)

### Global Methodological & Governance Notes
- **Survey Weights:** Every mean, share, and quintile is weighted strictly by `wgt_combined` (never unweighted averages). Raw sample counts (`n_unweighted`, `n_hh`, `n_students`) are always paired beside weighted totals.
- **Sector-Specific UMPCE Quintiles (`sector_umpce_quintile`):** `Q1 (Poorest)` through `Q5 (Richest)` are computed separately inside `Rural` and inside `Urban` (exact 20.0% population shares each) so urban price levels do not distort poverty tiers.
- **Thin-Cell Gate (`low_n_flag`):** `low_n_flag = 1` flags any cell with unweighted sample size `< 30`. Inside `1A4_nss_region_and_district_diagnostic_scorecard.csv`, domain metrics with `n < 30` are masked to `NULL` and excluded from District Stress Rankings.
- **Critical Raw-Data Notes:** (1) `hostel_exp` in Block 6 holds the **Hostel Destination State/UT code (`1–35`)**, not rupees, and is excluded from rupee wallet totals; (2) `nature_inst` (`Recognized`/`Unrecognized`) was canvassed strictly for `General + Private Unaided` students in Schedule 25.2 (dual denominators reported in the hidden-variables table (1A4_hidden_variables_unrecognized_english_mobility.csv)).

### Acronyms & Key Concepts Quick Reference
| Term / Abbreviation | Plain-English Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **NSSO 71st (Schedule 25.2)** | National Sample Survey Office 71st Round (*Social Consumption: Education*, Jan–Jun `2014`) — household demand, expenditure, coaching, and dropout microdata. |
| **OOP / Wallet Burden** | Out-of-Pocket private education expenditure; Wallet Burden = annual household education spend divided by annual total household consumption (`%`). |
| **UMPCE Quintiles (`Q1–Q5`)** | Usual Monthly Per Capita Expenditure quintiles (`Q1` Poorest 20% to `Q5` Richest 20%), computed separately inside Rural and Urban India. |
| **GAR / NAR** | Gross Attendance Ratio (all attending at a stage ÷ official age-group population); Net Attendance Ratio (age-appropriate attending ÷ age-group population). |
| **PM-POSHAN (MDM)** | Mid-Day Meal entitlement reported directly by student households. |
| **Within-Household Fixed-Effects (`FE`)** | Compares Rupee spending on sons vs. daughters inside the *exact same mixed-gender household*. |
| **`low_n_flag` (`n < 30`)** | Flags cells with fewer than `30` unweighted sample observations (masked to blank `NULL` in District rankings). |
