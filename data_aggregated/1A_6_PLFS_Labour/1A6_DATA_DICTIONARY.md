# Stage 1A.6 Data Dictionary — PLFS & NSSO Labour-Market Audit (2009–10 to 2025)

### Global Methodological & Governance Notes
- **Survey Weights (`final_weight`):** Every rate, share, quintile, and estimated headcount is weighted strictly by `final_weight` (never unweighted averages). Raw sample counts (`n_unweighted`, `n_youth_15_29`, `n_workers`) are always reported beside weighted figures.
- **PS vs. PS+SS vs. SS-Only:** `PS` captures Principal Status only; `PS+SS` adds Subsidiary-Only workers (`is_ss_only_worker = 1`, NOT working in PS but working in SS). Activity quality buckets (`Regular Salaried`, `Own-Account`, `Employer`, `Unpaid Family Helper`, `Casual Labour`) cover `100%` of `PS+SS` workers. Because NSSO/PLFS `2011–25` sets `nco_code_raw` and `nic_code_raw` to `NULL` for `SS_Only` workers, all NCO skill-tier and NIC industry shares are anchored strictly on `PS` workers across all 6 waves for apples-to-apples longitudinal validity.
- **2009–10 Exclusions & 2025 Revamped Frame:** `2009–10` (`NSSO_66`) has `usual_consumer_expenditure = NULL` (excluded from `UMPCE` quintiles) and uses `NIC-2004` (crosswalked for 6 broad sectors, excluded from `NIC-2008` Gig & Orange Economy proxies). PLFS `2025` (`revamped_2025_sample_flag = 1`) reflects MoSPI's expanded `270,472`-HH / `764`-district Calendar Year frame. Dedicated MoSPI gig-worker survey module launches Jan 2027.
- **Thin-Cell Gate (`low_n_flag`):** `low_n_flag = 1` whenever `n_unweighted < 30`. Inside `1A6_state_36_and_district_2025_adp_labour_diagnostics.csv`, district domain cells with `n < 30` are masked to `NULL` and excluded from District Stress Rankings.

### Acronyms & Key Concepts Quick Reference
| Term / Abbreviation | Plain-English Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **PLFS / NSSO** | Periodic Labour Force Survey (`2018–19` to `2025`) harmonized with NSSO 66th (`2009–10`) and 68th (`2011–12`) Rounds. |
| **PS / SS / PS+SS** | **Principal Status (`PS`)** = main activity over the past year; **Subsidiary Status (`SS`)** = secondary work (`>=30` days); **Usual Status (`PS+SS`)** = headline combined employment. |
| **LFPR / WPR / UR** | Labour Force Participation Rate (`%` working or seeking work); Worker Population Ratio (`%` employed); Unemployment Rate (`%` of labour force unemployed). |
| **NEET (`Codes 81, 92, 93, 94–97`)** | Youth Not in Employment, Education, or Training — split into Unemployed Searching (`81`), Domestic Duties Only (`92`), Domestic + Free Collection (`93`), and Disabled/Other (`94–97`). |
| **NCO / NIC** | National Classification of Occupations (skill tiers: Div `1–3` High-Skill White-Collar, `4–5` Mid-Skill, `6–9` Low-Skill/Manual); National Industrial Classification (industry sectors). |
| **UMPCE (`Q1–Q5`) / ROI** | Usual Monthly Per Capita Expenditure quintiles (separate within Rural & Urban); Return on Investment (controlled salaried/UR payoff from Formal Vocational training). |
